01 / ABOUT ME

Understanding biology through research and computation.

I am Kunwar Anshumaan Singh, a Biotechnology postgraduate from Bundelkhand University, Jhansi, India. My academic and research journey has focused on understanding biological systems through experimental and computational approaches.

I have gained research exposure at CSIR-Indian Institute of Toxicology Research (CSIR-IITR), Lucknow, in inhalation toxicology and computational exposure assessment, and at the Central Ayurveda Research Institute (CARI), Jhansi, where I worked on microbial quality evaluation of herbal raw materials and finished products.

My research work also includes molecular docking studies investigating the potential interactions of polystyrene micro/nanoplastic-related compounds with human biological targets, alongside systematic literature research on nanoparticle interference in common in vitro assays.

I am particularly interested in integrating molecular biology, bioinformatics, genomics and computational toxicology to investigate molecular interactions, disease mechanisms and emerging environmental challenges.
