01 / INHALATION TOXICOLOGY Research Project

Inhalation Toxicology Assessment of Lithium-Ion Battery Thermal Runaway Emissions via MPPD Modeling

Investigated the potential respiratory deposition of particles generated during lithium-ion battery thermal runaway using Multiple-Path Particle Dosimetry (MPPD) modelling.

Methodology
  • Particle collection and size characterization using cascade impactor-based methods.
  • Assessment of particle size distribution and aerodynamic parameters.
  • Respiratory deposition modelling using MPPD v3.04.
  • Comparison of regional deposition across age- and sex-specific lung models.
Technical tools
MPPD v3.04 MOUDI MMAD GSD SEM-EDX Aerosol Science
Research outcome

The modelling analysis showed differences in regional respiratory deposition across the evaluated lung models, with deposition patterns varying between respiratory regions and demographic model parameters.

Research conducted during internship at CSIR-IITR, Lucknow.

02 / MOLECULAR DOCKING In Silico Research

Mechanistic Pathway of Polystyrene Micro/Nanoplastics on Human Biological Targets: An In Silico Molecular Docking Study

Investigated potential molecular interactions between styrene tetramer, used as a representative polystyrene oligomer, and selected human biological targets.

Methodology
  • Selection of human proteins associated with biological and toxicological pathways.
  • Preparation of molecular structures and ligand parameters.
  • Molecular docking using SwissDock and Attractive Cavities scoring.
  • Interpretation of docking scores and predicted molecular interactions.
Technical tools
SwissDock SwissParam PyMOL Attractive Cavities Protein–Ligand Analysis
Selected findings

The reported docking free-energy estimates included -5.91 kcal/mol for IL-6 and -5.90 kcal/mol for TGF-β. These are computational predictions, not experimental binding measurements.

03 / NANOTOXICOLOGY Systematic Review

Nanoparticle-Induced Interference with Common In Vitro Assays: A Systematic Review

A systematic review investigating how nanoparticles can interfere with commonly used in-vitro assay methods and potentially influence the interpretation of experimental toxicity measurements.

Research approach
  • Systematic literature screening and study selection.
  • Extraction of nanomaterial and assay-related information.
  • Assessment of assay interference mechanisms.
  • Evidence synthesis across included primary studies.

04 / BIOINFORMATICS Computational Practice

Reference Genome Alignment and Sequence Analysis Using NGS Tools

Practical computational work involving a Saccharomyces cerevisiae reference genome, sequence alignment and downstream file-processing tools.

Workflow
  • Reference genome preparation and indexing using Bowtie2.
  • Alignment of sequencing reads against a reference genome.
  • SAM/BAM file processing using SAMtools.
  • Introduction to variant-processing workflows using BCFtools.
Bowtie2 SAMtools BCFtools FASTA SAM/BAM Linux/WSL

Computational learning and practical workflow development. Not presented as a completed whole-genome variant discovery study.
